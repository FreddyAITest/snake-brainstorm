## ⚠️ Red Flags (Avoid These)

### Don't Pursue If:
- "Everyone" is already doing it (logos, generic AI art)
- Race to bottom on price (Fiverr $5 gigs)
- No clear ROI for client (art for art's sake)
- Requires expensive upfront investment
- Legal/compliance uncertainty (trademarked content)
- Platform dependency risk (one algorithm change kills you)

### Green Flags (Pursue These):
- Clear business ROI (more sales, higher conversion)
- Repeatable/recurring revenue potential
- Underserved niche (specialization)
- Fast delivery possible (AI advantage)
- Low upfront cost (bootstrap-friendly)
- Multiple revenue streams (packages, retainers)
